## Motion rules

The release style allows one in-motion entrance per text block, then a static readable dwell. Text may scale from `0.96` to `1.00` or translate a short distance, but does not loop, oscillate, flicker, rotate continuously or use RGB-split. Cut and SFX events provide pace rather than shaking text.
